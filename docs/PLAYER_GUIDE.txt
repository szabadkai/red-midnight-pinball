RED: MIDNIGHT PINBALL — v1.0.0
https://github.com/szabadkai/red-midnight-pinball

CONTROLS
Left / Right: flippers and RED lane-light switching
Hold X or Down, then release: charge and launch
Z: nudge (three rapid nudges trigger tilt)
Hold Up: see upper playfield
Esc / Enter: pause, field guide, sound and motion settings
X at title: play; Z at title: field guide

Release X after starting a game, then hold it again to charge the first shot.
Full charge reaches the upper orbit. Hold a flipper to catch, release to roll,
then flip to aim. Eight seconds of lantern save follow each launch.

THE JOURNEY
1. Hit six blue bumpers to defeat the Wolf.
2. Shoot the glowing left cottage to rescue Grandmother and start multiball.
3. Hit red bumpers and lantern banks to break the Briar Hart.
Victory starts a harder journey. Three balls per game; best score persists.

BONUSES
RED lanes increase the multiplier to 5x. Two sets start three-ball multiball.
A RED lane within 2.5 seconds of launch awards a 2500 skill bonus.
Three ordinary scoop visits start multiball. Shoot the right well during
multiball for a 5000 jackpot. Complete lanterns for ball save outside the boss.

BROWSER BUILD
Extract the whole archive; open index.html and click Play.
Keep index.html and red-midnight.js together. If local files are restricted,
run python3 -m http.server 8000 in the extracted directory, then open
http://localhost:8000 in your browser.

PICO-8 CARTRIDGE
Drag red-midnight.p8.png into PICO-8, then type RUN. The editable .p8 file
contains the same complete game. Native PICO-8 authoring tools are not bundled.
